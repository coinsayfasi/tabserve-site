Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tabserve ("we", "us") handles information when you visit www.tabserve.com.tr (the "Site"). We keep things simple and collect as little as possible.

Information we collect

This Site is a static website. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect names, emails or payment details through the Site. If you email us, we receive only the information you choose to send.

Analytics & cookies

We use privacy-friendly analytics to understand aggregate traffic — such as which pages are popular and which countries visitors come from. This may set cookies or similar identifiers. The data is aggregated and is not used to personally identify you.

Advertising

We may display advertising on this Site in the future, including via Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Third-party links

The Site links to the App Store, Google Play and our app guide pages. Those services have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

Our apps

OneBag, Routevia and RentFlow have their own in-app privacy notices describing any data they process on your device. This policy covers the website only.

Children

The Site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Legal basis for processing

Where EU/UK data protection law applies, our legal basis for the limited processing described above is legitimate interest (understanding aggregate Site traffic, keeping the Site secure) or, for advertising cookies, your consent given via the cookie banner. You can withdraw that consent at any time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export the limited data associated with your visit, and to object to or restrict certain processing (for example, personalised advertising). Because this Site does not maintain user accounts, most requests reduce to opting out of analytics/advertising cookies via the banner or your browser settings. To exercise any other request, email teknopattv@gmail.com.

Data retention

Aggregate analytics data is retained only as long as needed to understand traffic trends and is not tied to an identifiable person. Advertising cookies follow the retention periods set by Google, typically up to 13 months. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to respond to your question.

International data transfers

Analytics and advertising services we use (Google) may process data on servers located outside your country, including in the United States, under Google's own data protection safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable).

How we protect your data

The Site is served entirely over HTTPS. We do not operate a server-side database of visitor data — the Site is static, and any analytics/advertising data is processed directly by Google under its own security practices.

California residents (CCPA)

We do not sell personal information. If you are a California resident and have questions about data collected via advertising cookies, you can opt out via the cookie banner or Google Ads Settings.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email teknopattv@gmail.com.
