Cookie Policy

Last updated: 1 July 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how www.tabserve.com.tr ("the Site") uses cookies and similar technologies. By using the Site you agree to the use of cookies as described here. You can withdraw consent at any time (see "Managing cookies" below).

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They help websites work, remember preferences and understand how the site is used.

How we use cookies

  • Essential — needed for the Site to function and to remember your cookie choice.
  • Analytics — privacy-friendly, aggregated statistics about which pages are popular and where visitors come from.
  • Advertising — we may display ads via Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

Third-party cookies (Google AdSense)

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (such as the DoubleClick/Google advertising cookie) to serve ads on this Site. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this and/or other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising via Google Ads Settings, and review third-party options at aboutads.info/choices and youronlinechoices.eu.

Managing cookies

You can accept or reject non-essential cookies via the banner on the Site. You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings at any time; note that some features may not work without them.

Cookie retention

Essential cookies typically expire when you close your browser or after a short session. Analytics and advertising cookies set by Google may persist for up to 13 months, after which they expire automatically and are reset the next time you visit and consent again.

Do Not Track signals

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. There is no common industry standard for how sites should respond to DNT, so the Site does not currently change its behaviour based on this signal — instead, use the cookie banner or your browser settings to manage cookies directly.

Mobile apps

This Cookie Policy covers www.tabserve.com.tr only. Our mobile apps (OneBag, Routevia, RentFlow) do not use browser cookies; any analytics or advertising identifiers used within the apps are described in each app's own Privacy Policy, linked from its store listing.
